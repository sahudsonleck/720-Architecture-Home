#### The Delta-T ($\Delta T$) Optimization Formula:
Energy consumption is directly tied to the work required to maintain the temperature gap between the room and the interior:
$$\Delta T = T_{\text{ambient}} - T_{\text{internal}}$$

By shifting the node to a winter garage ($40^{\circ}\text{F}$ ambient) instead of a heated kitchen ($72^{\circ}\text{F}$ ambient), the compressor workload is mathematically reduced by over 40%.

#### Mandatory Engineering Safeguards for Garage Deployment:
* **Dual-Environment Multi-Sensors:** Prevents the system from shutting off when ambient external temperatures drop below freezing.
* **Variable-Capacity Linear Compressors:** Capable of reversing cycles to act as a mild internal warming mechanism if external temperatures drop to extreme sub-zero levels, preventing internal inventory spoilage.
* **Vacuum-Insulated Panels (VIPs):** Replacing standard polyurethane foam to deliver 5x thermal resistance at 1/5th the physical wall thickness.
